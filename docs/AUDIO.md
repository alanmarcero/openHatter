# openHatter v2 audio notes

## Voice

Each hit excites an inharmonic six-mode metallic bank. The four mode choices use separate partial shapes: band-limited pulse and noise for the 909 model, softened phase-dispersed modes for Anjuna Silk, evolving cross-modulation for the uplifting FM bell, and asymmetric pulse modes for the 808 model. Per-hit phase offsets and per-partial decay rates keep repeated hits from collapsing into the same phase-locked tone. The tuned root, octave, cents and pitch chick still control the modal register.

The air layer generates one of four noise textures, shapes its attack, hold and decay, then applies serial high-pass and low-pass filtering. Stick bounces arrive before the main hit with independent amplitude and alternating pan. A chick transient provides the primary stick, foot or laser contact. One-shot ADSR is applied after these dry layers; its release is bounded by the dry tail, and the reverb tail continues separately.

## Effects path

Exciter saturation and tilt feed a parallel-knee compressor, stereo peak limiter and a four-line feedback reverb with input diffusion and high-pass filtering. Reverb damping and the choke envelope shape its tail. Host transport determines the reverse capture length for Beat End, Beat 4 Auto, half-beat and quarter-beat modes. Master Mid/Side width and output gain follow the processing chain. Lock WAV captures and plays back the rendered instrument hit in the current session.

The new preset bank is aimed at progressive off-beats, 138–140 BPM uplifting trance, classic Dutch trance, tech-trance, psy-trance, and clean club utility. Individual presets vary the metal body, transient, air texture, ADSR, saturation, dynamics, stereo image and space. All factory entries set each presettable parameter.

## Useful starting points

- For a short kick-pocket hat, reduce Air Decay and Metal Decay, then keep a fast ADSR release and moderate Air HPF.
- For an uplifting off-beat, raise the metal octave, set a clear pitch chick, use the FM Bell or Silk body, and widen the air layer.
- For a darker tech-trance hat, lower the air LPF, shorten bounce spread, add a little exciter drive, and tighten the reverb choke.
- If the transient is too soft, reduce ADSR Attack and raise Chick Level before increasing overall output.
